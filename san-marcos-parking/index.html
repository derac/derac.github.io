Primary source and important exceptions

City GIS: Parking zones ↗ and mapped individual spaces ↗. Only entries coded FreePublic are counted in these layers. The layers may be incomplete or outdated; park lots without a FreePublic record need another source.

Downtown limits: The City describes 2-hour and 4-hour street parking, with time limits Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. It also identifies these 4-hour blocks: 300–400 E San Antonio; 100–200 N Edward Gary; 100–200 Martin Luther King; 300–400 and 400–500 W San Antonio. These block descriptions are not drawn as exact curb boundaries here. Official 4-hour-block notice ↗ · Downtown regulations ↗.

City Park: current city fees and permit criteria ↗. Free only for approved local-area permit holders; others have a first-30-minute free period, then fees. Open 6 a.m.–11 p.m.; no overnight parking.

Rio Vista / Children’s Park: seasonal access rules ↗. Summer 2027 access rules apply on listed dates from May 29 to September 6. See the affected lot details for parking changes.

Park lots: City park parking lot GIS ↗ maps lot locations, not individual stalls. This layer has no fee or time-limit field. Greenbelt parking inventory ↗ provides additional trailhead context.

OSM: San Marcos map area ↗ and license / contributors ↗. OSM fee and access tags are crowdsourced, not regulatory advice.

Older long-stay lead: Downtown Association’s former Armory tip ↗ is not included as currently verified until GIS or on-site signs confirm access and hours.
